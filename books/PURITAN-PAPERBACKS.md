# Puritan Paperbacks

The Banner of Truth's *Puritan Paperbacks* (all 64, in the order
https://puritanpaperbacks.com/puritan-paperbacks-set gives them), worked
through one at a time. Our edition is always the full early text, taken
from the source (CCEL or EEBO-TCP); Banner's copies are a guide to the
selection, not a source. Many Banner titles, Owen's especially, are abridged or
renamed, so each book's original title and extent is settled when we take
it up.

Status: **done** (the editor has cut a release tag for it), **merged** (finished
and in main, waiting for the editor's tag), **in review** (built, questions
open), **next**, or blank.
