# Witnesses: this edition, the 1650 printing, Monergism

Three texts of *The Glorious Feast of the Gospel* compared word by word:

- **1650**: the first edition (London, John Rothwell), EEBO-TCP A93248, the
  base of this edition (`A93248.tcp.xml`), read through the machine pass
  (spelling modernized, nothing else changed).
- **this edition** (`chapters/typ`): first published from a lightly
  modernized text of the kind printed in Grosart's *Works* (1862), then
  corrected against 1650 (below). Every difference from 1650 is an editor
  decision in the TEI.
- **Monergism**: Monergism's PDF (from the SDG reprint, (c) Monergism Books
  2017), a modernized 1650 text. A check only: none of its text is used.

## Corrected (October 2026)

Where the earlier copy departed from 1650 and Monergism agreed with 1650:

- 60 misprints from a scanned source, e.g. Gentles → Gentiles, "the church
  lied into the wilderness" → fled, sold → soul, bead → head, "we have united
  for him" → waited, Tertulhan → Tertullian, he/be and b/h confusions,
  "their goods things" → good things, "give me of that bread" → us.
- To the Reader: about 300 words the copy had lost are restored after "the
  best of the best" ("Here is variety and plenty too…" through "…besides the
  marriage feast."), with "the hidden manna" before Revelation 2:17.
- Greek (To the Reader): αὐτόπιστοι, ἀξιόπιστοι, Ἀληθῶς … καὶ ἀληθῶς,
  Κατῆλθεν … ἀθάνατος, καὶ τῷ θανάτῳ καθεῖλε.

A check of the whole text then corrected: unclosed or misplaced quotation
marks (To the Reader, sermons 1, 6, 8), stray punctuation (",.", ".;",
"'.,", "....."), spacing ("feast ,", "Genesis 49:20 ."), references
(Romans 11:20, 21; 1 Kings 17:4, 6; 2 Corinthians 5:13, 14; Philippians
2:9; Esther 3:8), Latin (Laetitia, lachrymae, praemissa, audacter,
haerebit, ludus, optimus, disertos), and colours, derideth, vouchsafeth,
camphire, poisonful.

## Still different from 1650 and Monergism

Left as the edition has them; the earlier copy's editor may have meant
them, and none makes nonsense. Spelling and modernization (labours,
Saviour, it is for its, St for Saint, you for ye, Zion), scripture
references and the "Obs."/"Use"/"Ans." labels are not listed.
