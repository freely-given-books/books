The University of Nebraska-Lincoln's *Electronic Texts in American Studies* 57 (ed. Reiner Smolinski)
reproduces the 1734 first edition with its preface; it refuses scripted downloads, so the preface's
gap fills (by sense) wait to be checked against it or another copy of the 1734.
