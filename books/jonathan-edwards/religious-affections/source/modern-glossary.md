# Religious Affections: Modern English glossary

How the Modern English edition renders words that meant something else to Edwards (false friends). The editor's rule
(2026-11-16): such a word is replaced with a modern word in the text, chosen by context, never glossed; the sentence and
Edwards's voice stay. Scripture quotations (BSB as adjusted), verse and the Classic are untouched. General rules:
`MODERN-ENGLISH-RULES.md`; change list: `work/false-friends-2026-11-16/religious-affections.md`.

## Kept (Edwards's own terms, or not shifted)

affections, exercise(s) (Edwards defines the affections as "exercises of the inclination and will"), natural man / men
(1 Cor 2:14), evangelical, animal spirits (period physiology he explains in place), the **earnest** of the Spirit (the
word of 2 Cor 1:22 / 5:5 / Eph 1:14 he expounds, in the quotations as adjusted), experiment (test), hypocrites, divines,
dispensation(s), immanent. **humiliation** waits on the editor (Vikunja project 18 #49).
