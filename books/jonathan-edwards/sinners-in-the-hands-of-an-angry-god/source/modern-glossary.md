# Sinners in the Hands of an Angry God: Modern English glossary

How the Modern English edition renders words that meant something else to Edwards (false friends). The editor's rule (2026-11-16): such a word is replaced with a modern word in the text, chosen by context, never glossed; the sentence and Edwards's voice stay. Scripture quotations (BSB as adjusted) and the Classic are untouched. General rules: `MODERN-ENGLISH-RULES.md`; change list: `work/false-friends-2026-11-16/<book>.md`.

| Edwards | Modern English |
|---|---|
| the mere pleasure of God (the doctrine) | the sheer good pleasure of God (rules §3.2) |
| God's mere will | God's will alone |
| arbitrary (will, mercy) | unconstrained, sovereign |
| willing (to show his wrath, Rom 9:22) | determined, intends |
| secure (in health, quiet and secure) | unconcerned |
| careful (lest) | concerned |
| resent (the earth does not resent it) | is not angered by |

Kept: natural man / men (Edwards's term, 1 Cor 2:14), regard, security where it means safety.
