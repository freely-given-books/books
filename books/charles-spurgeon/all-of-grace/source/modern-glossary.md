# All of Grace: Modern English glossary

How the Modern English edition renders words that meant something else to the author (false friends). The editor's rule (2026-11-16): such a word is replaced with a modern word in the text, chosen by context, never glossed; the sentence and the author's voice stay. Scripture quotations (BSB as adjusted), verse and the Classic are untouched. General rules: `MODERN-ENGLISH-RULES.md`; change list: `work/false-friends-2026-11-16/<book>.md`.
