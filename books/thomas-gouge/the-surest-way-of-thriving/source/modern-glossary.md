# The Surest and Safest Way of Thriving: Modern English glossary

How the Modern English edition renders words that meant something else to the author (false friends). The editor's rule (2026-11-16): such a word is replaced with a modern word in the text, chosen by context, never glossed; the sentence and the author's voice stay. Scripture quotations (BSB as adjusted), verse and the Classic are untouched. General rules: `MODERN-ENGLISH-RULES.md`; change lists: `work/false-friends-2026-11-16/<book>.md` and the 2026-11-17 re-check, `work/modern-recheck-2026-11-17/surest-way/surest-way-recheck-edits.py`. Settled by the editor and kept (rules §7b): "thy flesh" → flesh; "owned him" → "was not ashamed of him"; wages.
