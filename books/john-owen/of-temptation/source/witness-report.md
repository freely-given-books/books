# Witnesses: Goold's printings and the 1658 first edition

The edition is CCEL's text of *Of Temptation* (`temptation.thml.xml`),
which is William H. Goold's (*The Works of John Owen*, vol. 6, Johnstone and
Hunter, 1850–53). It was checked against:

- **Goold's own printing**, the archive.org scan of vol. 6
  ([worksofjohnowe185006owen](https://archive.org/details/worksofjohnowe185006owen)),
  through its OCR;
- **the American reprint of Goold's text**, *On Temptation and the
  Mortification of Sin in Believers* (Philadelphia, Presbyterian Board of
  Publication, n.d.), archive.org
  [ontemptationmort00owenuoft](https://archive.org/details/ontemptationmort00owenuoft),
  reset in new type, through its OCR;
- **the 1658 first edition** (Oxford, for Tho. Robinson), EEBO-TCP A90277,
  kept untouched in `A90277.witness.xml`.

None of the scans is kept here. William H. Gross's modernized text (On the
Wing, © 2015, published by Monergism) was read only for sense; it is under
copyright and not kept here.

## Corrections to CCEL

CCEL's words were compared with both Goold scans and with 1658. CCEL's
text of this treatise is careless: where CCEL differs from Goold and 1658
agrees with Goold, CCEL is wrong, and the edition now has Goold's words.
Where Goold himself departs from Owen's 1658 wording (both Goold printings,
or the 1850 one corrected in the reprint), the editor's rule is Owen's
word over Goold's. All are editor decisions in the TEI (`review-report.md`).
The 1658 edition's spelling is not followed: the edition stays in Goold's.

Also restored from the 1658 comparison (CCEL lost them; Goold has them):

| Where | Restored |
| --- | --- |
| ch. 4 | that it *must needs be past all doubt, to him who is so assaulted,* that it is a peculiar power of temptation |
| ch. 5 | the means of it so efficacious and powerful,—*the entrances of it so deceitful, subtle, insensible, and plausible,*—our weakness |
| ch. 7 | the beauty *of holiness, as it is designed by Christ for the end, issue, and effect* of his death |

and Owen's words where both Goold printings lack them:

| Where | Goold | Owen 1658 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ch. 8 | The prince of this world came upon him, every thing in earth or hell … | came upon him, *the glory of this world was showed unto him*, every thing … |
| ch. 8 | for his sake and his God | for his sake and his *good* |

Kept as Goold has it: ch. 3, “able to deliver the godly out of
temptations” (the King James wording of 2 Pet. ii. 9, as Owen quotes it in
chapter 2; 1658 “temptation”). “judgement” (twice) is spelled “judgment”
as in the rest of the book.

## The 1658 edition

Compared word by word with `colophon/drift.py` (spelling, case and
punctuation ignored):

```sh
drift.py source/of-temptation.tei.xml source/A90277.witness.xml \
    --name "1658 edition" --div dedication=to-the-reader.typ \
    --div chapter=chapter-01.typ,...,chapter-09.typ --min 3
```

What remains is mostly Goold's: references written out, the Greek the TCP
transcription left out, the chapter summaries (Goold's: 1658 has none), and
small changes of wording; Goold also follows the 1658 errata.
Differences of fewer than three words are counted but not listed.
