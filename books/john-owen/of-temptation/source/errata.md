# Of Temptation: the 1658 errata and the TCP's slips

The orig layer is the 1658 first edition as EEBO-TCP A90277 keys it. No free 1658 page images
exist, so the TCP stands as our witness of the print (the editor, 2026-11-05). Corrections go
into the reading text (reg) only, never into orig.

## The printer's errata (1658)

The TCP keys the errata leaf (`front/div[@type="errata"]`, not printed in this edition). Each
entry was checked against the TCP page it names and the reading text there
(`work/of-temptation/errata/`, 2026-11-05). Goold's text, the review, already follows nearly all
of them; several "variants" the 1721 collation found (body/bowels, turfe/trifle,
carrying/concerning, how/what, will/with, those/these, returnes/returners) are these errata,
not later revisions.

lost in the TCP); p. 51 "even, add of", p. 65 "leads", p. 66 "dele succour" (the only

page.

## Keying slips in the TCP (collation with 1721 and Goold)

`work/of-temptation/collate-3m/TRIAGE.md`: 15 places where the TCP reads nonsense and 1721 and
Goold agree. Orig keeps the TCP (with no 1658 page, a keying slip cannot be told from a
misprint); the reading text has the correct word, from the review:

will/with (an erratum, above), which/withe, the missing "it" (that it useth), thir/their,
int/into, solicitatious/solicitations, exaspertes/exasperates, hypocrities/hypocrites,
solticious/solicitous, ambitition/ambition, meanining/meaning, distempet/distemper,
Contentmens/contentments, specedily/speedily; distenper and tenptation are macrons
(`MACRON_M`).
