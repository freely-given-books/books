# The Mortification of Sin: Modern English glossary

How the Modern English edition renders words that meant something else to Owen (false friends). The editor's rule (2026-11-16): such a word is replaced with a modern word in the text, chosen by context, never glossed; the sentence and Owen's voice stay. Scripture quotations (BSB as adjusted) and the Classic are untouched. The general rules are `MODERN-ENGLISH-RULES.md` at the repository root; the change list is `work/false-friends-2026-11-16/<book>.md`. Wordings the editor settled (rules §7b: unsettles, takes blow after blow, effectual) are not reopened.
