# Institutes of the Christian Religion (Calvin, 1559) — sources

Started 2026-10-11. Henry Beveridge's translation, in six volumes.

- **Base:** CCEL `institutes.thml.xml` (https://www.ccel.org/ccel/c/calvin/institutes.xml),
  sha256 `d7995178c1fe1723d1124126010e430f12d0444239ad7f9a7c8c818aa9766717`.
  Beveridge's translation (Calvin Translation Society, Edinburgh, 1845), keyed by
  CCEL from the 1953 Eerdmans reprint (its page breaks are the `pb`s). About
  700,000 words; Greek kept, Hebrew transliterated by Beveridge (none in CCEL).
  Known CCEL slip: "For the word () is more properly to be understood" (a lost word).
- **Left out** (the editor's call, 2026-10-11): John Murray's introduction to the 1953
  reprint (in copyright), Norton's 1561/1581 prefaces and title page, the 1953
  tables and indexes, Olevian's "Method and Arrangement", the general index of
  chapters, and the hundred Aphorisms. Kept: Calvin's prefatory address to Francis
  and his three prefaces to the reader (1539, 1545, 1559).
- **Witness, the 1845 printing (archive.org, University of Toronto copies):**
  `instituteschrist01calvuoft`, `institutesofreli02calvuoft`, `institutesofthec03calvuoft`
  (vols 1-3); their OCR kept here as `*.djvu.txt` (git-ignored). Page images:
  `https://archive.org/download/<ID>/page/n<N>_w1600.jpg`. Other copies:
  `institutesofchrbeve01calv`, `institutesofchrbeve03calv` (1845); `instituteschris00calvgoog` (1863).
- **Monergism:** `institutes.monergism.pdf` (2,067 pages, © Monergism Books):
  Beveridge's text, but with chapter titles that read as Battles's 1960 ones (in
  copyright). A witness to the body only; git-ignored, never copied.
- **sermonindex.net:** `/books/the-institutes-of-the-christian-religion/` mirrors CCEL
  (its first page is CCEL's "A New Translation by Henry Beveridge, Esq"); not a
  separate witness. `institutes-1.sermonindex.htm`, git-ignored.
- **Not used:** Norton's 1561 translation (EEBO-TCP A17662), a different
  translation; John Allen's (1813).
