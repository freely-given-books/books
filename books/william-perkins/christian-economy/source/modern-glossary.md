# Christian Economy: Modern English glossary

How the Modern English edition renders words that meant something else to Perkins (false friends). The editor's rule
(2026-11-16): such a word is replaced with a modern word in the text, chosen by context, never glossed; the sentence and
Perkins's voice stay. Scripture quotations (BSB as adjusted) and the Classic are untouched. General rules:
`MODERN-ENGLISH-RULES.md`; change list: `work/false-friends-2026-11-16/christian-economy.md`.
